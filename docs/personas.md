# User Personas

## Persona 1 — Everyday Owner

### Goals

- Maintain one vehicle
- Avoid unexpected repairs
- Receive reminders
- Track expenses

### Pain Points

- Missed maintenance
- Lost receipts
- Forgotten renewals

---

## Persona 2 — Family Manager

Owns multiple vehicles shared among family members.

Goals:

- Shared ownership
- Driver assignments
- Centralized records
- Shared reminders

---

## Persona 3 — Car Enthusiast

Goals:

- Complete maintenance history
- Modification tracking
- Performance records
- Vehicle analytics

---

## Persona 4 — Workshop Manager

Goals:

- Manage appointments
- Upload service history
- Increase repeat customers

---

## Persona 5 — Insurance Agent

Goals:

- Improve renewals
- Digital policy delivery
- Better customer engagement
