# Business Model

## Executive Summary

The Digital Car Ownership Platform is a B2C-first platform with a long-term ecosystem strategy. Revenue is generated through premium subscriptions, marketplace commissions, partner integrations, and business services while delivering free core functionality to attract a broad user base.

---

# Customer Segments

## Primary

- Individual car owners
- Multi-car households
- Car enthusiasts

## Secondary

- Automotive workshops
- Insurance providers
- Car dealerships
- Fleet operators

---

# Value Proposition

For users:

- One place for every vehicle
- Reduce ownership costs
- Protect resale value
- Never miss maintenance or renewals

For businesses:

- Reach engaged vehicle owners
- Increase customer retention
- Offer digital services
- Gain operational insights

---

# Revenue Streams

## Freemium Subscription

Free:

- One vehicle
- Basic reminders
- Service history
- Document storage

Premium:

- Unlimited vehicles
- Family sharing
- Advanced analytics
- Cloud backup
- Smart reminders
- AI recommendations
