### Suggested Plan Structure
| Plan | Vehicles | Price (Monthly) | Price (Annual) | Key Features |
|------|----------|-----------------|----------------|--------------|
| **Free** | 1 | $0 | $0 | Core tracking, 1 vehicle, email reminders, export |

### Feature Gating Strategy
- **Free**: 1 vehicle, core features, base AI/storage
