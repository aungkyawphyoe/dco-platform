Built for every type of driver

One application for everything related to vehicle ownership — save time, save money, never miss important deadlines, and protect your resale value.

  • ⚡
    Electric Vehicle Owners
    • Charging costs — log every kWh and what you paid at the plug.
    • Energy use — monitor consumption trends across drives.
    • Real cost per km — true ownership cost beyond the sticker price.
  • ⛽
    Petrol & Diesel Drivers
    • Track fuel efficiency — volume, spend, and frequency in one log.
    • Monitor service history — every receipt and odometer reading.
    • Organized maintenance — plan items surface the next due job.
  • 👪
    Families With Multiple Cars
    • Manage multiple vehicles — every car in one shared garage.
    • Share records — service and cost history anyone can find.
    • Stay organized — switch the active vehicle in one tap.
  • 🏢
    Fleet Owners
    • Track multiple vehicles — across unlimited cars in one garage.
    • Monitor usage and costs — per-vehicle fuel, service, and repair totals.
    • Centralize vehicle data — one source of truth for the whole fleet.
  • 🚗
    Ride Share & Delivery
    • Fuel & earnings costs — track spend against what each trip made.
    • Mileage for tax deductions — automatic odometer log per shift.
    • High-mileage maintenance — reminders tuned to heavy use.
