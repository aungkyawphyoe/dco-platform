## Set up your garage

Your garage holds a separate set of records for each vehicle. The selected vehicle determines which costs, upcoming services, and history appear on the dashboard. Give similar vehicles distinct names so you can tell them apart at a glance.

1. Connect your phone to the internet and open AutoX. Sign in if you already have an account.
2. For a new account, choose **Create account**, enter your email, enter your password twice, and submit the form. Follow the email verification prompt.
3. On the first-vehicle screen, tap **Add vehicle**. If you skipped this step earlier, open the vehicle selector on **Garage**, open **My Garage**, and choose the add option.
4. Enter a name you recognize, such as “Family car.” Add the vehicle details and current mileage, then tap **Save**.
5. Return to **Garage** and check the selected vehicle name. To record something for another vehicle, switch vehicles here first.

## Record a fuel stop or charge

A fuel entry keeps the quantity and cost together. For example, if you buy 30 liters for 90,000 kyat, enter 30 as the quantity and 90,000 as the cost. These entries feed the vehicle’s refueling statistics.

1. Select the vehicle you just filled or charged.
2. Tap **Refuel** or **Charge**, depending on the vehicle.
3. Check the date. Enter the quantity from your receipt in the unit shown, then enter the cost. For a fuel stop, select the fuel type.
4. Add the odometer reading if available and tap **Save**.
5. Open the vehicle’s fuel records and check the date, quantity, and cost against the receipt.

## Track another expense

Use an expense for a payment such as parking. The category explains what you paid for, while the date determines which month includes the cost in your statistics.

1. Select the vehicle and open **Expenses**.
2. Check whether the cost already appears, especially if you just logged fuel or a service.
3. Choose the add option. Select a category, enter the payment date and amount, and save the expense.
4. Find the entry in the list and open it to check the details.

## Record a completed service

The maintenance plan tells you what is due. Service history tells you what actually happened. Link the completed job to its plan item so your next reminder reflects the work you just recorded.

1. Select the vehicle and open **Maintenance**.
2. Check the maintenance plan for the work you had done, such as an oil change.
3. Choose **Register service**. Select the matching plan item when available so the app updates its schedule.
4. Enter the service date, odometer reading, and cost from the invoice. Save the service.
5. Open the service history to check the entry. For planned work, return to the plan to check when the item comes due again.

## Share a vehicle

Sharing lets someone use their own AutoX account to access a vehicle in your garage. An invitation by email is useful for a specific recipient. A QR code is convenient when you have both phones together.

1. Open the vehicle’s sharing controls and choose the code or QR option. A QR code is the square pattern another phone can scan.
2. Give the intended recipient the eight-character share code or show them the QR code.
3. Ask them to open **Join with code** in their shared vehicles and enter the code or scan it.
4. Check the vehicle’s sharing list to see who has access.

> ℹ️ **Note** · A share code expires after seven days. **Regenerate code** immediately invalidates the previous code. Give the recipient the new one.

## Recover your password

Use the email reset if your account has a password and you can still open its email inbox. The reset code lets you choose a new password without knowing the old one.

1. Connect to the internet. On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address for your account and request the reset code.
3. Check your email. Enter the code in AutoX, enter your new password twice, and tap **Reset password**.
4. Sign in with the new password.

## Plan the next service and set reminders

A plan item describes recurring work, such as changing the oil after a certain distance. A reminder gives you time to book the work before it comes due.

1. Select the vehicle and open **Maintenance Plan** from the app menu.
2. Choose a suggested service or add a custom item. Enter its interval using the time or distance fields that apply and save it.
3. In **Settings**, open **Reminders**. Set **Days before due** for calendar-based work and **Distance before due** for mileage-based work.
4. For an email reminder, verify your email address, enable **Email reminders**, and set the lead time. When the work is done, record the completed service against the plan item.

## Keep paperwork and part numbers with a vehicle

Use **Documents** for paperwork you may need to show someone, such as an insurance policy. Use **Parts** for details you want to reuse on the next service, such as the exact oil filter that fits.

1. Select the vehicle, open the app menu, and choose **Documents**. Open **Add Document**, give it a recognizable name, and choose a category.
2. Tap **Add file** and choose the camera, photo library, or a PDF. Add an expiry date if the document has one. Save and reopen the entry to check that the file is legible.
3. To keep a part number, open **Parts**, choose **Add Part**, and enter the name. Add the brand, part number, and any fitment notes, then save.
4. When recording a service, select the saved part to show which one you used.

> ⚠️ **Warning** · Deleting a document removes its attached file. Keep a separate copy of paperwork you need to retain.

## Understand your running costs

1. Select the vehicle and open the app menu. Choose the view that matches your question.

   | Your question | Where to look |
   | --- | --- |
   | How much fuel or charging have I recorded? | **Refuel Stats** summarizes fuel entries for the vehicle. |
   | What have I spent on servicing? | **Maintenance Stats** draws on completed service records. |
   | How much did I spend during a particular period? | **Expense Stats** summarizes recorded expenses. |

2. Select the year or month you want to review. If a period is empty, clear the filters and check the dates of the original entries.

## Adjust your account and display preferences

| When you want to… | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Change the app language | Open **Settings → Localization** and select your preferred language. |
| Match the units on your receipts or dashboard | Open **Settings → Unit and Format**. Check the currency and distance unit before entering figures. |
| Change the screen theme | Open **Settings → Appearance** and choose the display mode you find easiest to read. |
| Check how you sign in | Open **Settings → Account connections** to see which sign-in methods are linked. |
| Check your vehicle allowance | Open **Plans & subscriptions** and review your current plan before adding a vehicle. |
| Keep a private reference | Open **Notes** and save a short reminder. Copy anything important elsewhere before replacing your phone. |

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A saved record seems to be missing | Check the selected vehicle, then open the relevant history and check the date. |
| Changes are waiting to sync | Reconnect to the internet, open **Sync status**, and tap **Sync Now**. Check the status again before switching devices. |
| You cannot add another vehicle | Check your plan’s vehicle allowance in **My Garage**. |
| You need help finding an action | Open **Help** and replay the guided tour. |

> ⚠️ **Warning** · Clearing app data or uninstalling AutoX can erase changes that still need to sync. Private notes stay on this phone even after a successful sync. Copy important notes before changing phones.
