## Sign in and find your vehicle

The fleet administrator gives your account access to specific vehicles. Your selected assignment determines which vehicle receives your inspection, fuel entry, or issue report. Check the plate each time you switch vehicles.

1. Ask your fleet administrator for your fleet email address and temporary password.
2. Connect to the internet, open AutoX, and sign in with those details.
3. At the first sign-in prompt, enter your temporary password as the current password. Enter and confirm your new password.
4. Open **Garage**. If you drive more than one vehicle, use the vehicle selector to choose today’s assignment.
5. Open **Vehicle details** and match the plate to the vehicle in front of you.

## Complete a trip inspection

An inspection is a set of checks your fleet asks you to perform before or after a trip. Each answer records the condition you actually observed. A failed check gives the repair team a work order to follow up.

1. Park safely. With the correct vehicle selected, tap **Start Inspection**.
2. Choose **Pre-trip** before the journey or **Post-trip** afterward. Select the checklist your fleet uses, then tap **Start inspection** to open its items.
3. Check each listed item on the vehicle. Mark **OK** if it passes or **Not OK** if you find a fault. Complete all required items.
4. Add notes that help someone locate the fault, then tap **Submit inspection**.
5. Check the result in **Reports** under **Inspections**. A failed inspection creates a work order, the fleet’s record for tracking a repair.

> ⚠️ **Warning** · If you find a fault that makes the vehicle unsafe, keep it parked and contact your fleet supervisor before driving.

## Record a fuel stop

The fuel log pairs the amount bought with the price paid and the vehicle’s distance reading. Use the figures from the same stop so your fleet can relate fuel use to distance traveled.

1. Select the vehicle you filled and tap **Refuel**.
2. Use your receipt to select the fuel type and check the date.
3. Enter the quantity and cost in the units shown. Check the odometer field against the vehicle’s total distance reading.
4. Save the entry and check the vehicle’s fuel logs.

## Report a problem and track the repair

Use an issue report when you notice a problem between inspections. The description tells the repair team what you observed, the odometer places it in the vehicle’s history, and urgency helps the team prioritize.

1. Select the vehicle and tap **Report Issue**.
2. Check the vehicle name. Enter the odometer reading in kilometers and select the issue type.
3. Describe what happened in at least 10 characters. For example, write “Left headlight stays off.” Select the urgency.
4. Tap **Submit report**. Open **Reports**, then **Work orders**, to check the report and follow its status.

> ℹ️ **Note** · Use **Report Issue** to tell the fleet about a problem. Entries in **Notes** are private to your account on this phone.

## Record maintenance

Use a service record when work has already taken place. It gives the fleet a dated history of what was done, at what mileage, and at what cost.

1. Open **Maintenance** for the selected vehicle and choose **Register service**.
2. Identify the service you completed. If you opened the form from a plan item, check that it matches the work.
3. Enter the service date and mileage. Add the total cost, notes, and any parts used.
4. Tap **Save** and check the service history. If a problem still needs a repair, use **Report Issue** to alert the fleet.

## Read your reports after a shift

1. Open **Reports → Inspections** to find a submitted checklist. **Passed** or **Failed** describes the inspection result at the time of the check.
2. Open **Reports → Work orders** to follow a reported fault. **Reported** means the issue is logged, **In progress** means the fleet has started work, and **Completed** means the fleet has recorded completion.
3. If a completed repair develops the same fault again, check the vehicle plate and report your new observation with the current odometer reading.

## Find vehicle records during a shift

| When you need… | Where to look and what it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Upcoming work | Open **Maintenance Plan** for the selected vehicle. Use its due information to coordinate servicing with your supervisor. Your organization manages the plan. |
| A receipt or vehicle paper | Open **Documents** and select the named document to view its file. Connect to the internet if the file is stored in the cloud. |
| A replacement part number | Open **Parts** and look for the component name. Check its brand and part number before using it in a service record. |
| A summary of costs | Open **Refuel Stats**, **Maintenance Stats**, or **Expense Stats** in the app menu. Check the selected vehicle and time period before comparing totals. |
| A private reminder | Tap **Notes**. Notes stay on your phone. To tell the repair team about a fault, submit an issue report. |

## Check whether a saved entry reached the fleet

1. Open the app menu and tap **Sync**. **Pending Items** lists changes still waiting on your phone.
2. Reconnect to the internet and tap **Sync Now**. **All changes synced** means the queue is empty.
3. Check **Submission review** if your entry needs an administrator’s decision. **Waiting for Fleet Admin** means it awaits review. **Accepted by Fleet Admin** or **Declined by Fleet Admin** tells you the decision. If you need help with a declined entry, give the administrator the vehicle and record date.

> ⚠️ **Warning** · Keep the app and its data on the phone while entries are pending. Clearing app data can erase unsent records.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No assigned vehicle** or the wrong plate | Check the vehicle selector. If the correct vehicle is missing, give its plate and your fleet email to your administrator so they can check the assignment. |
| **No inspection templates** | Ask your administrator to create a checklist for your fleet, then reopen the inspection. |
| The issue report will not submit | Enter a nonnegative odometer reading and a description of at least 10 characters. Check your connection and look in **Reports** before submitting again. |
| You forgot your password | Connect to the internet, tap **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen, request the email code, and use it to set a new password. |
| Records are waiting to sync | Reconnect, open **Sync status**, and tap **Sync Now**. Check the status before clearing app data or changing phones. |
