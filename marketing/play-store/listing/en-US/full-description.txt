AutoX is a digital garage for the vehicles you actually drive.

One calm place for what your car needs next, what it has cost, and what you have already done — kept on your phone and ready when you are.

YOUR GARAGE
• Add a vehicle with plate, make, model, year, mileage and fuel type
• Switch the active vehicle in one tap; every tab follows it
• Archive a car without losing its service history

MAINTENANCE AND REMINDERS
• Build a maintenance plan from suggested items or your own
• Set reminders by date and by mileage
• Register a service with cost, parts and notes, and keep the full history

FUEL AND CHARGING
• Log refuels and charging sessions with litres or kWh, price and odometer
• See consumption, cost trends and cost per distance in Fuel Stats

EXPENSES, PARTS AND DOCUMENTS
• Record spending by category and see monthly totals
• Keep a parts list for each vehicle
• Store documents, photos and PDFs against the right vehicle

STATS
• Fuel, maintenance and expense charts with year and month filters

NOTES
• A private notebook for plate numbers, workshop contacts, anything
• Notes stay on your device and are never uploaded

SHARING
• Share a vehicle with a partner, family member or mechanic
• They see the records you choose, and you can remove access at any time

WORKS OFFLINE
• Garage, maintenance, expenses, fuel logs, documents and notes work without a connection
• Changes sync when you are back online; signing in needs a connection
• Reminders are scheduled on your phone

FOR PERSONAL OWNERS AND FLEET DRIVERS
• The same screens serve a private garage and an assigned fleet vehicle
• Drivers sign in with an account from their fleet admin and see their assigned vehicles

LANGUAGES AND APPEARANCE
• English and Burmese
• Light, dark or system theme

PLANS
• Free: 1 vehicle, 1 active share, 50 MB document storage
• Lite: 3 vehicles, 3 shares, 150 MB
• Standard: 10 vehicles, unlimited shares, 500 MB
• Fleet: unlimited vehicles and sharing, 5 GB (organizations)
View and change your plan in Setting → Plans & subscriptions.

Your records live on your device first, so the app keeps working when the signal does not.
